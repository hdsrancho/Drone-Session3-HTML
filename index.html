01

What is 3D designing?

Creating a digital model that has length, width and height, so it can be viewed from any side, tested, animated or turned into a real object by 3D printing.

Engineering

Aircraft, cars, robots and machine parts are modelled before they are built.

Games and film

Characters, worlds and effects are all 3D models.

3D printing

A model exported as STL becomes a physical part, layer by layer.

Simulation

Models are tested in virtual wind tunnels and stress tests before manufacture.

Workflow

1. Sketch

Idea and rough dimensions

2. Model

Combine shapes into a 3D form

3. Test

Simulate flow, loads, fit

4. Export

STL for printing or CNC

Various 3D design tools and their roles

Tinkercad

Browser based, built from basic shapes. Best first tool for beginners and 3D printing.

Fusion 360

Parametric CAD with sketches and constraints. Product and mechanical design.

SolidWorks

Industry CAD for parts, assemblies and engineering drawings.

FreeCAD

Open source parametric modeller for engineering work.

Blender

Free mesh modelling, sculpting, rendering and animation.

OpenFOAM / Ansys

Simulation tools. They run airflow (CFD) on a finished model, like our wind tunnel.

04

Parts of an aircraft and their roles

Every part has one main job. Change a part and the flight changes. Session 2 showed how they control roll, pitch and yaw.

Fuselage

Carries crew, cargo, fuel and engines. Its shape sets the drag.

Wings

Make the lift that holds the aircraft up.

Ailerons

Flaps on the wings that roll the aircraft left or right.

Elevator

Moves the nose up or down (pitch).

Rudder

Turns the nose left or right (yaw).

Engines

Give the thrust that pushes the wings through the air.

Types of wings and their roles

06

Where each wing is used

Trainers and light planes

Straight

Cessna 172, Piper Cub

Slow, forgiving and high lift at low speed.

Regional turboprops

Straight

ATR 72, Dash 8

Efficient on short trips and short runways.

Gliders and high flyers

Long, thin straight

ASK 21 glider, U-2

Long wings give very little drag from lift.

Airliners and business jets

Swept

Boeing 737, Airbus A320, Gulfstream G650

Cruise close to Mach 0.8 with low drag.

Fighters

Delta or swept

Mirage 2000, MiG-21, MiG-29

Fast, strong and able to fly at high angles of attack.

Supersonic and space flight

Delta

Concorde, Space Shuttle

Big area gives lift at the high angles used to slow down.

Try them in the simulator: Light plane (straight), Airliner and MiG-29 (swept), MiG-21 and Mirage 2000 (delta).

07

Wing details that change performance

Aspect ratio

Gliders, U-2 and fighters

Long thin wings waste less energy. Short wide wings are strong and turn quickly.

Winglets

Boeing 737 MAX, Airbus A320

They weaken the wingtip vortex and cut fuel use. Watch the orange tip vortex in the simulator.

Dihedral and anhedral

Cessna 172, C-5 Galaxy, Harrier

Wings angled up steady the roll. Angled down balances high-wing aircraft.

Flaps and slats

Every airliner

Extended for takeoff and landing so the wing gives enough lift at low speed.

08

Wind-tunnel Simulator

  • Air flows left to right and wraps around the body, splitting at the nose.
  • Wingtip vortices form, roll up and sink behind the model.
  • Set the angle of attack from -25 to +25 degrees.
  • Past about 13 degrees the wing stalls and a turbulent wake grows.
  • Drag for a full 360 degree view.
Blue smooth flow, orange vortex, red turbulent wake
